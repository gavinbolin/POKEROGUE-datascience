Farm Encounter Rules

Each encounter has a chosen option and a fallback if that option is unavailable. The fallback starts as the leave option, or the last option when there is no leave. Shady Vitamin Dealer is the pattern: the pricey deal, otherwise leave. Turn on Leave to always take the last option. A follow-up dropdown or checklist appears when the chosen option asks for a Pokémon, an item, or a sub-option. Simple conditions, when they fail, also use the fallback. Saving backs up the old file. The farm reloads it on its own.

Other encounters catch knobs, not mystery encounters
